Location lets your agents ask where you are. When a thread or bot needs it, for a nearby café, the weather outside or how long the trip home takes, sand asks your phone for its position and hands the agent the latitude, longitude and accuracy.

## Good for

- "Find a pharmacy open near me"
- Weather and travel times without typing your address

## How it picks a device

sand asks every phone and browser that has sand open. A phone's answer is used straight away. If a browser answers first, sand waits a moment for a phone before using the browser's. When nothing answers within 15 seconds, the agent is told your location is unavailable.

## Privacy

Your phone or browser asks you once for location permission. After you allow it, agents get your precise location whenever they ask, without another prompt. Turn the add-on off, or take location permission away from sand in your phone's settings, to stop sharing it.
